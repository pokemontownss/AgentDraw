The collection rules are fixed in the contract and cannot be changed:

  • Supply: 1,444 — 1,222 Free Draw + 222 Instant Draw, two separate pools with separate counters. Neither pool ever blocks the other.
  • No phases: one mint switch opens Free and Paid at the same moment. Paid is drawable from the first second mint opens.
  • Free Draw: 0 BNB, max 2 per wallet, Binance Agent ID holders only — the contract checks the registry live at draw time. One global queue: a free draw must wait at least 90 seconds after the previous free draw by anyone. Free draws roll DOODLE 70% / PIXEL 30% only — never a rare tier.
  • Instant Draw: open to every wallet — Agent ID holders included (they may use both paths). It never checks for an Agent ID and never looks at the Free pool. Price starts at 0.0012 BNB and steps up +0.0003 BNB for every 500 total minted. The style tier is rolled inside the draw transaction: DOODLE 27% · PIXEL 25% · NEON 20% · RARE 12% · GLITCH 10% · GOLD FOIL 5% · MASTERPIECE 1%.
  • No pre-numbering: token IDs follow mint order from one counter. Each token's data file is chosen by its rolled tier: doodle.json · pixel.json · neon.json · rare.json · glitch.json · gold-foil.json · masterpiece.json.
  • All fees stay in the contract and the owner can withdraw the entire balance at any time — no lockup, no conditions (step 05).
  • Mint starts CLOSED. Open it in step 03 when you are ready — opening it opens Free and Paid together.
