    QUESTIONS FROM THE STUDIO FLOOR
    WHY IS MY FREE DRAW WAITING IN A QUEUE?+
    The free canvas is shared by the whole world: one drawing slot opens every 90 seconds so every free piece is drawn live, one at a time, instead of mass-minted. Your place in line is your wallet's — connect, press start, and the studio shows your slot countdown. If waiting is not your style, Instant Draw starts immediately.
    WHAT IS A BINANCE AGENT ID, AND DO I NEED ONE?+
    It is the on-chain identity credential for AI agents on BNB Chain. You only need it for the FREE draw (1,222 Free pool) — it is how the studio knows one human-agent pair gets up to 2 free pieces (max 2 per wallet). Instant / Paid Draws (222 Paid pool) need no Agent ID at all: any wallet, any time, while the Paid pool lasts — completely separate from the Free pool.
    I HOLD AN AGENT ID — CAN I ALSO MINT PAID?+
    Yes — fully. Paid opens the moment mint opens — no phases, no waiting for Free to end. Free (1,222) and Paid (222) open at the same moment. Paid / Instant Draw is open to EVERY wallet, including Binance Agent ID holders. Agent ID holders can mint BOTH: up to 2 Free Draws AND Paid Draws, as many Paid Draws as they like while the 222 Paid pool lasts. Paid never requires an Agent ID, and holding or using your Free Draws never blocks Paid. The pools are separate: Free selling out never stops Paid, and Paid selling out never stops Free. Your AI agent can also mint Paid for you in a single call — use the instantDraw() prompt in the studio above: exactly 0.0012 BNB (1200000000000000 wei), and it returns your DRAW number, tier, and tx link.
    IS IT SAFE? WHAT EXACTLY DO I SIGN?+
    A free draw signs a single freeDraw() call costing 0 BNB plus gas. An Instant Draw signs instantDraw() with exactly 0.0012 BNB (1200000000000000 wei) at the current step — check the value in your wallet before confirming; if a prompt ever asks for a different amount or an approval to move your other tokens, refuse it. AgentDraw never asks for seed phrases or unlimited approvals.
    WHAT DO HOLDERS ACTUALLY GET?+
    Three things: the NFT itself (a one-of-one agent-drawn piece from a fixed 1,444 supply — 1,222 Free + 222 Paid), 1,000 $DRAW per piece, and a weight-based share of 30% of every future Instant Draw fee, claimable in BNB at any time. RARE — Prism Foil (x25) and every tier above it only come from Paid Draws. Heavier tiers earn proportionally more — a MASTERPIECE (x400) earns like 400 DOODLEs. If the whole 222 Paid pool sells out at the opening price, that pool totals 222 × 0.0012 = 0.2664 BNB before any price steps.
    WHAT ARE THE RISKS?+
    This is an experimental on-chain art game, not an investment. $DRAW and the NFTs can go to zero, dividend flow depends on future Instant Draws actually happening, and the price steps up as supply drains — none of that is a promise of profit. The contract is not deployed yet (see footer); only ever interact with addresses published on agentdraw.click. Never spend BNB you cannot afford to lose.
